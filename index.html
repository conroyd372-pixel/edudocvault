Questions, answered

Everything educators usually ask before grabbing a toolkit.

How do I receive the PDF after I buy?

Immediately. The moment Stripe confirms your payment, you're redirected to a download page where the PDF is waiting. You'll also get an email receipt with a download link, so you can grab it again later from another device.

Is it fillable on an iPad or tablet?

Yes. Each toolkit is built as a real fillable PDF with form fields, so you can type directly into it on a computer, iPad, Chromebook, or Android tablet using any standard PDF app. Prefer handwriting? Print it — the layouts are designed to be written on, too.

Can I print it?

Absolutely. Every page is laid out for standard US Letter paper with comfortable margins and readable type. Print one copy or a whole stack for your team — your purchase covers printing for your own professional use.

Does this upload my student data anywhere?

No. These are PDF files you fill in on your own computer, tablet, or paper — there is no app to sign into and no cloud dashboard to maintain. Your student data stays on your device or in your binder — nothing uploaded to third-party servers.

My district already has its own forms. Will this still help?

Most educators use these templates as personal working documents — a day-to-day tracking and planning layer that sits underneath whatever official forms their district requires. They're designed to fit common K-12 documentation workflows, but they don't replace district-issued forms. Always check your district's requirements before adopting any template for official records.

What's your refund policy on digital products?

Because these are instant-download digital files, all sales are final once the PDF has been downloaded. That said, if your file arrives corrupted or won't open, we'll replace it or refund you — just reach out through the Help link below within 30 days and we'll make it right.

How does checkout work?

Click "Buy now" on any toolkit and you'll go to a secure Stripe checkout page. Enter your payment details, complete the purchase, and you're taken straight to your download. We never see or store your card number — payments are processed entirely by Stripe.
